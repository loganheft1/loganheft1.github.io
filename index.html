About

Technical depth.
Product mind.

I was a data scientist first where I've shipped real models in production. I've grown up alongside technologies like LLMs as they emerged, building with them rather than presenting about them. I've dealt with the scrutiny from Risk and Legal teams as they pick apart technical architecture that they struggle to understand. I've learned from some of the sharpest technical minds, and that is why today I'm responsible for the models that underwrite a multibillion dollar credit portfolio.

I first studied at New Jersey Institute of Technology in the Albert Dorman Honors College , majoring in finance and computer science. NJIT is one of only a few schools on the East Coast with men's volleyball, and I was fortunate enough to receive a scholarship to play. After graduating in only three years, I went on to Brown University where I earned my MSc in Data Science. Whilst there, I worked at MITs Lincoln Laboratory building NLP intelligence infrastructure for a Department of Defense research program that must stay appropriately classified. I later joined PNC as a Data Scientist and was tapped for the TPM role eight months later, stepping into a seat that would typically be filled from an MBA pipeline. My background is why I can sit with engineers and pressure-test model architecture in the morning, then translate the same conversation into business risk and return for a credit committee in the afternoon.

Outside of work

When I'm not working, the place I'm most likely spending my time is running — training upwards of 70 miles a week. I'm a competitive marathon runner, currently preparing to run 2:45 this April at the Jersey City Marathon. It will be my eighth.

I also have a creative side in photography. My subjects are mostly runners, but I shoot street photography and landscapes from my travels as well. My work has been featured by brands like New Balance and by influencers with millions of followers.

Logan Heft is a Technical Product Manager at PNC Bank in New York where he owns the AI-driven credit decisioning platform for consumer and business credit cards. He is currently architecting Project Meridian, an agentic AI portfolio surveillance system using LLM reasoning loops and Kafka signals. Outside of work Logan is a competitive marathon runner training for a 2:45 finish at the Jersey City Marathon, his eighth marathon, and a photographer whose work has been featured by New Balance. Logan is open to senior Technical Product Manager and AI Product Manager roles in fintech, banking, AI infrastructure, and consumer technology.
